# Fixed-to-Triad reuse audit

Read-only audit at `59f1a949`. Conclusion: Fixed inference work helps Triad as
an address/schedule and qualification library of patterns, not as a drop-in
kernel or proof of equal numerics. No speedup is claimed here.

## Boundaries and evidence

- Profile inputs: `profile-cuda132/nt-d768-captured.json`,
  `profile-cuda132/tn-prism-captured.json`, and their raw/details/SASS exports in
  this directory. These diagnose the current AUTO symbols; profile duration is
  not admission timing.
- The six ordinary direct TF32 tiles are already recorded losers in
  `.superpowers/sdd/handoff-codex-gemm-bi-triad-2026-09-05/triad-next-candidate-inventory.md`;
  do not resweep them as a substitute for the staging experiment.
- `kernels/gemm_bi_triad/sm80_tn_splitk.cu` offers a k-major copy pattern, but
  its fused fixed reducer changes association. The reachability/numeric-family
  boundary is recorded in `.superpowers/sdd/handoff-codex-gemm-bi-triad-2026-09-05/triad-split8-reachability-audit.md`; it is not a
  preserve-association replacement for either profiled direct route.
